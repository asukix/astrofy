# The System behind Feynman's techniques

Richard Feynman was a great thinker. I like his attitude and aspects about thinking and learning. In this article I would like to organize his system from my own perspective. In my approach I separated his technics for 3 part:

- **Learning:** first principal
- **Validating:** simple sentence
- **Teaching:** compression sentence

In my opinion this can be a whole system because it start from learning something new. After learning He not just learned a topic he also validated his understanding. And a validated understanding is not the end of the rode. When you give back what you learnt than can be a good system.

## 1. Learning

The curiosity is part of human nature. We like questions, we like understanding and we like to explore. Our system is programmed for this.

Everyone is curious in their own way we just have to give it time to enjoy this attitude. When everything is rushing, when answers come in seconds we don't have time for thinking.

Curiosity is natural. As a child, I completely disassembled several clocks to understand how they worked because I was curious.

Here is Feynman's First Principles technique. When you learn something keep asking until you reach an answer that is simple and straightforward.

In my opinion, in the modern environment, if we ask and are curious, we usually get an answer. However, I think we often don't really understand the answer, even if we pretend to understand it.
