## Creation and destruction
When I am trying to understand the thinking space, I am creating my own mental model. This is quite easy. The hard part for me the destruction of the model. I found sometimes the destruction can be also quite easy.

## Difficulties in destruction
I noticed 2 tipe of difficulties during I rebuild my mental model
1. When I create my mental model from scretch with understanding the problem space.
2. When I used a model that I learnt but it not realy mine.

### Destruction of my realy own mental model
When I build my mental model from ptoblem space is so hard for me to destroy it. It is hard because I made it. I spent **a lot of time to understand the problem and create a solution.** This process make hapiness and after this I feel: ok I am done, this is looks good and this is what I made. And thats why I feel this is mine and thats why the destruction is hard, and that's why I don't want to do it. But if I do It the new model usually better because my understanding improved.
Lets give a real word example. If I create a lego house that I imagine. I can build it and I enjoy the building of the house. When it is ready it feels good, but the building was the most enjoyable part. After that I don't want to destroy it. But when I do it and rebuild again it will be better and I also can feel the hppyness of the building

### Destruction a build in mental model
After I learnt something and I used it a lot of time and I used it as a base, and build new thing to it that is **a built in model** for me. But it is a learnt built in model not a built one. 
When I try to understand it's background and it's problem space I try to make my own solution. At this situation I noticed I use my old understanding and I just recreate a very similar model. And this is the hard part, because I can't forget the solution that I learnt earlier. It is good because I can understand the model better but it is hard, because it is not my own model. That is not a problem. We can use others model bacuse we don't have time to rebuild everything from scretch. But I am talkink about my own models.

### Learnt model vs built model
I found an important dinstinction between a learnt model and a model that I built from scretch. If I built my mental model, I can rebuild my solution anytime and it can be easier changed and it can easier evolve. When I use a learnt model I can just use it and I had to remember to it. I can't build it. I can't evolve it. And tell the truth I feel that **I don't realy understand it.** That's why I prefere my own models.

### Questions make it easier
What I love in Thinking Space System is that I have time thinking and I have time to understand. Understand the problem and make my own solution. During this process my most powerful tool is my curiousity and asking questions. I found that I stucked with destruction questions helps me. And the interesting here the more questions make more curiousity that creates more questions. And this process help me to change my mind and not concentrate realy to the destraction even in building. And the building process automatically destroys the old model.

#### Architecture example
Let check an architecture example together with MVVM. And let me add very short answers because of the example. MVVM is the Model View ViewModel. *But what is the model?* The model is the place of the business logic. And here some more questions as example:
- What is the difference between the model and the model in view model? - The model in view model shouldn't contain business logic or calculation.
- How I connect the model, the view and the view model? - One connection is that the View contains the VM and the VM contains the Model.
- Is there other elemts in the MVVM? - Yes, the converter that can be used by the View.

### Explanation and structed thinking
When I try to rebuild an existing not my own mental model that is built in it also hard to me. I noticed something durin I shared my thoughts and during I was writing my own articles. When I try to explain something it helps me to find my gaps. And it also help me to try to understand the problem again and that indicates to rebuild my model. And here a connecting technique:
Have you ever tried to explain something to someone and found the issue that person didn't undersand it? What I did here that I tried to explain it from a different aspect. And this helps me to also rebuild my learnt model. And this is also an enjoyable way, because I can make my brain to work, because I give it a problem. And this time the destruction is easier to me.

## Recap
The destruction can be easy even it is my mental model even it is a learnt and built in model. The questions and new aspects helps me to concentrate to the rebuilding and enjoy it's process instead of concetrating to concentrate to the destruction.
My favorite part of writing my articles that they helps me to understand better and helps me to enjoy the thinking and helps me to destroy my existing models.
