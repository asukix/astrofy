## Thinking at the center
I like learning and I like thinking and thinking about thinkin. Yeah I know this might sound a litle strange but I like it.
During learning I always ask questions about what I learn. One time I asked question: What if I didn't learn the answers, but rather understood the problem space itself? And what if I tried to find the answers myself?
This can be sound that it can take a very long time. Currently we can get answers and with AI we can get them more faster. When we get answers so quickly, it can be strange to slow down. And we don't need it most of the time.
What we get if we slow down and try to understand the problem space? We will have space for learning. We ill have time **to enjoy thinking**. We will have time **to discover ansvers** and we will have time to **injoy the way** instead of just getting the goal.
This is what **I love in thinking**: I have time to go on my way, make my own mistaskes, to learn from my mistakes and understand the word, the systems better around me with this.
